## The short version

Hedgehog Bits does not collect, store or share any personal data. We run no servers for the app, so nothing you put in it ever reaches us.

Your vault is encrypted on your iPhone and stays there, unless you choose to copy it somewhere: with optional sync, with your iPhone backups, or as a backup file you export. Wherever a copy goes, it stays encrypted, and it opens only with your password.

## The app

- **No data collection.** The app has no servers of ours, accounts, analytics, crash reporting services or advertising. We never receive your files, your password or anything about what is in your vault.
- **Storage on your iPhone.** Notes, photos, videos and files you add are encrypted on your iPhone. By default they are left out of iCloud and computer backups.
- **Game scores.** Personal best scores are stored on the device, like any casual game.
- **Names typed at the start screen** are kept in memory only and never saved.

### Copies you choose to make

Each of these is off until you turn it on. Only encrypted files leave your iPhone, encrypted with keys that come from your password and never leave the device.

- **Sync (optional, part of Hedgehog Bits Plus).** **Settings → Sync** keeps the vault the same on your devices, through one of these:
  - **iCloud:** the vault is stored in a private area of *your own* iCloud account that only the app can use. Apple stores it under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and it counts against your iCloud storage. We have no access to it.
  - **A folder you choose** in the Files app, such as Google Drive, Dropbox or a drive at home. The company that provides it stores the files under its own privacy policy. We have no access to it.

  The storage provider can see that there are some files, how many, roughly how big they are and when they change. It can't see file names, folder names or anything inside them.
- **iPhone backups.** If you turn on **Settings → Include in iPhone Backups**, the encrypted vault is included in your iCloud or computer backups, which Apple or your computer hold as they do the rest of your iPhone's data.
- **Exported backups.** If you export a backup, the encrypted file goes only where you choose to save it. We never receive it.

### Feedback and ratings

- **Feedback.** **Settings → Feedback** opens an email in your mail app. It includes your app version, iOS version and iPhone model, and nothing from your vault. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you do, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply and to improve the app. We don't share them, and we'll delete them if you ask.
- **Ratings.** The app may ask whether you'd like to rate it. Ratings and reviews are handled by Apple, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Permissions

The app asks for these only when you use the related feature:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | To take photos and videos, and to scan documents, straight into the vault without saving them to your photo library. |
| Microphone | To record sound when you capture a video into the vault. |
| Photo library | To import items and, if you choose, remove the originals after they are safely in the vault. |

### Purchases

Hedgehog Bits Plus is sold through the App Store, and Apple handles the payment. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app checks your purchases on your iPhone with Apple's StoreKit. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### When the app connects to the internet

Only to check purchases with Apple and, if you turn on sync, to reach your own iCloud or the folder you chose. It never connects to us.

### Deleting your data

You are in control of every copy:

- **On your iPhone:** erase the vault in **Settings → Erase Vault**, or delete the app.
- **The synced copy:** **Erase Vault** can delete it too. You can also remove it yourself: for iCloud, in your iPhone's **Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage**; for a folder, by deleting its **Hedgehog Bits** folder.
- **Backups:** delete the iPhone backups or exported files that hold it.

Deleting the app doesn't remove the synced copy or any backups. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over.

### Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law (UK GDPR and GDPR) you have rights over personal data held about you. Hedgehog Bits does not collect any personal data through the app. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to access, correct, export or delete those emails.
