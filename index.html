Private vault for iPhone

Your files, encrypted on your iPhone and nowhere else.

Hedgehog Bits keeps notes, photos, videos and documents in an encrypted vault on your device. There are no servers, no accounts and no sync, so your data stays in your hands. To anyone else, it looks like a small collection of brain games.

How it works

One question at the door

The app opens with a hedgehog and a single question: “Who is playing?” Your answer decides what happens next.

  1. Type your vault password and the vault opens. The key is derived from your password on the device, then used to decrypt your files in memory.

  2. Type anything else and the app greets you by that name and offers ten short games. Nothing hints that a vault exists.

  3. Leave the app and the vault locks immediately. The app switcher shows only the hedgehog.

Ownership

You hold the only key

We designed Hedgehog Bits so that we never have access to your data. Not because of a policy we promise to follow, but because there is nothing on our side to access.

Stays on your device

Everything is stored on your iPhone. The vault is excluded from iCloud and computer backups and from phone-to-phone transfer.

No servers, no accounts

No sign-up, no email address, no sync service and no analytics. The app does not need a network connection to work.

Your password, your key

The encryption key comes from your password. There is no recovery option and no back door, for you or for anyone else.

Backups you control

Export the vault as one encrypted file and keep it wherever you choose: a Mac, a USB drive or a folder of your own.

Inside the vault

Room for what matters

Organise items in folders, move them with drag and drop, and open them without leaving the app.

Security

Standard, well-studied cryptography

We use established algorithms with conservative settings and document how they fit together.

Argon2id

Your password is turned into a key with Argon2id, using 256 MiB of memory and 3 passes, which makes guessing slow and costly.

AES-256-GCM

Content is encrypted in authenticated 1 MiB chunks. Tampering, reordering and truncation are detected.

Separate keys

Each file, thumbnail and the folder index gets its own derived key, under a random 256-bit master key.

No plaintext at rest

Files are decrypted in memory only. Rare temporary copies are deleted on close and on lock.

  • No cloud storage
  • No sync
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No third-party keyboards
  • No password recovery
  • No back doors
