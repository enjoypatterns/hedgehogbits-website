What the demo doesn't show

  • The encryption itself. Your password is turned into a key with Argon2id, and each item is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The security design page explains how.
  • Backups. You can export the vault as one encrypted file and restore it on a new iPhone. See moving your vault to a new iPhone.
  • The limits of a disguise. The games help against a passing glance. They are not what protects your data. What a disguise can and cannot do covers this.

More detail is on the features page and in the FAQ.
