## Request a feature

Tell us what you are trying to do and what would help. The reason behind a request is often more useful to us than a specific solution.

<p><a class="button button-secondary" href="mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}?subject=Feature%20request%3A%20">Email a feature request</a></p>

## Report a bug

Tell us what you expected to happen and what happened instead. If you can, include:

- the steps that make it happen again
- your iPhone model and iOS version (*Settings → General → About*)
- the app version (shown on the App Store page, or in *Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Hedgehog Bits*)

<p><a class="button button-secondary" href="mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}?subject=Bug%20report%3A%20">Email a bug report</a></p>

## Keep your vault private

You never need to share anything from your vault to report a problem.

- **Never send your vault password.** We will never ask for it, and we couldn't use it to help you: nothing is stored on our side.
- **Don't send backup files or vault contents.** Describe the problem in general terms, for example "a 2 GB video stops playing halfway", rather than sending the file.
- **Check screenshots before you attach them.** Blur or crop anything personal first.

## Report a security issue

If you think you have found a security vulnerability, email [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}?subject=Security%3A%20) with "Security" in the subject line. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix it before sharing details publicly. Our [security design]({{ '/security/' | relative_url }}) page describes how the app works, and [`security.txt`]({{ '/.well-known/security.txt' | relative_url }}) has the same contact details in a standard format.

## Things we can't help with

- **Forgotten passwords.** There is no recovery, by design. We have no copy of your password or your files.
- **Recovering a deleted vault** without an exported backup.

## What happens next

We reply when we can. Feature ideas are collected and considered together, so a request still counts even if it doesn't arrive straight away.

Before writing, the [FAQ]({{ '/faq/' | relative_url }}) may already have your answer.
